Documents due for review

These documents are Effective and their Next Review Date is within 60 days (or has passed), and you were an approver in their approval workflow (or you uploaded them, for a document that has no workflow). If nothing in a document needs to change, Re-approve it: no workflow runs and the version stays the same; the review is recorded and the Next Review Date moves on by the document type's Review Years. To change a document, revise it from Create / Update Document instead.

Re-approve the selected document(s) without any change? The version stays the same and the next review date moves on.
